The awkward part of recording a script is keeping your place while you’re trying to sound like yourself. TeleCue gives you a scrolling script you can read at a steady pace, with controls for the text size and where your eyes should rest on the screen.

You can paste a script or open a plain-text or Markdown file, then set a pace in words per minute. The iPhone and iPad prototype also offers mirror mode for teleprompter glass and keyboard controls for pausing, restarting, and changing speed. The apps share the same scrolling engine and reading-time calculations.

## Where it stands

There’s a packaged Mac build and an iPhone and iPad prototype. The mobile project has passed simulator tests. The next milestone is to rehearse a real recording with the Mac build and test the mobile app on physical devices before preparing submissions for the Mac App Store and the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

## Project updates

**27 November 2026.** The Mac build and mobile prototype give us a starting point for release testing. The mobile project still needs device signing and a first App Store submission.
