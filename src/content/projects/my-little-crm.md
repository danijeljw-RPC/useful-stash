A support call ends with a reference number and a promise to call back on Friday. When Friday comes and goes, can you find the number, remember who you spoke to, and tell the next person what was agreed? My Little CRM gives you one place to keep that record.

Log the conversation against a person or organisation, write down the details you’ll need next time, and set a follow-up. It’s built for everyday things like dealing with a utility provider, chasing a repair, or keeping track of a refund. The core app keeps your records on your device and works without an account or internet connection.

## Try it

My Little CRM is available for Android on Google Play.

<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.repasscloud.mylittlecrm"><img src="/images/google-play-badge-en.png" alt="Get My Little CRM on Google Play" width="168" height="65"></a>

The browser demo starts with sample contacts, conversations, and follow-ups. You can edit them and try the workflow. Refreshing the page resets the data, so use the demo with fictional details.
