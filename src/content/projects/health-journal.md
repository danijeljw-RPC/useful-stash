It’s hard to reconstruct several weeks of symptoms from memory when someone asks when they started, how often they happened, or what you were taking at the time. Health Journal gives one person a place to record those details as they happen and bring a timeline to an appointment.

The app is designed to work offline on iPhone and Android, with records stored in an encrypted database on the device. The aim is to make it free, without ads, subscriptions, or an account. An encrypted backup is intended to let you restore your journal on a replacement phone. The journal records what you enter so you can review and share it with your clinician.

## Where it stands

Health Journal is in development. The browser demo is designed around fictional records that reset on refresh. On Android, testing has covered saving and reopening records across a restart, exporting a fictional CSV, and resetting the journal. Physical-device testing and release checks are still ahead.

## Project updates

**27 November 2026.** Android builds are available for testing within the project. The next milestone is to verify the experience on real devices, including unlocking the journal and restoring an encrypted backup on a replacement phone.
